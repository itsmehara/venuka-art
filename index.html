A little about Venuka

Venuka loves to draw. Animals, birds, funny characters, whole little farmyards — whatever the idea is, it starts with a pencil and ends up full of colour.

She films the drawings so other children can follow along, and shares them on her YouTube channel, Happy Learning Kids. In her own words on her first website: “Teamed up with Dad to bring these videos to life, adding an extra layer of creativity to each drawing.”
