What each craft will show

The page is already built for them. When a craft is added it appears with:

  • A real photograph of the finished piece
  • Its title and a short description
  • The materials that went into it
  • Simple step-by-step instructions, where they help
  • A link to the video, if there is one
  • An Available on Request or Contact Me button
