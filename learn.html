Simple drawing steps

How almost every drawing here was made

Watch any of Venuka’s videos and you will see the same five steps.

  1. Start with the big shape. A circle for a head, an oval for a body. Draw it lightly — you will change your mind.
  2. Add the small shapes. Ears, legs, a beak, a tail. Keep them simple; you can always make them fancier later.
  3. Give it a face. Two eyes and a curved smile turn a shape into a character. This is the moment it comes alive.
  4. Go over the good lines. Press a little harder on the lines you want to keep, then rub out the rest.
  5. Colour it in. One direction, light first, darker after. Save the background for last.
