Privacy Notice

A short, plain-language explanation of what happens when someone uses the contact form.

Who should use the form

The contact form is intended for parents, guardians, teachers, and other adults. Children should use it only with a parent or guardian. Never include a child’s school, home address, phone number, passwords, or other private details.

Information collected

If the form is connected and you choose to send a message, it collects the name, email address, subject, message, consent confirmation, and the time received. The site does not ask for age, school, address, telephone number, or payment information.

Why it is collected

The information is used only to read the enquiry, prevent abuse, and reply when appropriate. It is not sold and is not used for advertising.

Where it is stored

Messages are stored in a private Google Sheet controlled by the site owner. Google processes that information as the service provider. The Sheet must not be shared publicly or set to “anyone with the link.”

How long it is kept

Messages should be deleted when they are no longer reasonably needed for the enquiry or reply. The site owner should review and remove old messages regularly.

Cookies and video embeds

The website does not use advertising cookies. YouTube players are loaded from the privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com domain only after a visitor chooses to play a video. YouTube may then process information under its own privacy terms.

Your choices

You do not have to use the form. To ask about a submitted message or request its deletion, use the contact details published on the Contact page. If no working contact address is shown, do not send personal information.

Last reviewed: 30 August 2026. This notice should be reviewed whenever the form provider, analytics, or information collected changes.
